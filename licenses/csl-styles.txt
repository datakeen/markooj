Citation styles shipped with Markooj (folder csl/, installed as resources/csl)

  apa.csl                  American Psychological Association 6th edition
  ieee.csl                 IEEE
  chicago-author-date.csl  Chicago Manual of Style 18th edition (author-date)

Markooj hands one of these to Pandoc for DOCX, ODT, EPUB, LaTeX and other exports, following
References -> Citation style. The files are used unmodified, as included with the Pandoc 3.14
source (test/command/apa.csl, test/command/ieee.csl, data/default.csl). They come from the
Citation Style Language styles project: https://github.com/citation-style-language/styles
