# Recover unsaved work

Markooj keeps a copy of the text of every open document while you work. If you close a document without saving, or Markooj or your computer stops suddenly, the text is still there.

## Get your text back

1. Click **File → Recover**.
2. Each entry shows the document's name, when it was last changed, and the first lines of its text.
3. Click **Open** to open the recovered text in a tab. If the same document is already open, Markooj asks whether to replace its text or open the recovered text as a separate copy.

If the original file still exists, **Compare** shows the differences between the recovered text and the file on disk, line by line, before you open anything.

The **×** button deletes one entry, and **Delete all** clears the list. Markooj asks first, because deleted entries cannot be brought back.

After a crash, a small note on the status bar says that text was recovered. Click it to open File → Recover.

## How long text is kept

Recovered text is kept for **7 days** by default. To keep it longer, choose 14, 30 or 90 days in **File → Options → Recovery**. Older entries are removed automatically.

## Where it is kept

The copies are plain files in a folder of their own in your user profile (or in the `data` folder of a portable copy). **Open recovery folder**, on the Recover page or in Options, shows the folder. They never leave your computer.

## Recovery is not saving

Recovered text is a safety net, not a replacement for saving. Press **Ctrl+S** often. The tab shows a dot while a document has unsaved changes.
