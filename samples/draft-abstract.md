# Abstract

In this study we discuss about the effect of an unsteady flood wave on the transport of gravel in a laboratory flume, and in order to do this a series of nine runs were carried out by the team with a stepped inflow that was designed to be very unique and that is different than the inflows used in earlier studies of the same kind.

The results show that transport on the rising limb is higher. The data was collected by a sediment trap at the end of the flume. Basically the method is really simple, and its easy to repeat in any lab that has a tilting flume.

We think that the findings may possibly be useful for engineers who comprise of river managers and designers.
